# CoinTracker

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

CoinTracker helps people browse cryptocurrency market data, keep favorites, inspect coin details, and compare coins. The user confirmed the coin-tracker purpose and requested restrained frontend UI and UX polish of the existing project.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing implementation: Vue, Vite, Pinia, Vue Router, Tailwind CSS, and DaisyUI.
- The current interface is English and displays market values in USD from CoinGecko.
- Search filters coins by name; market filters cover favorites and positive or negative daily changes.
- Favorites and theme preferences are stored locally in the browser.
- Coin details show price, market cap, daily high/low, all-time high, and market-cap rank.
- Comparison requires at least two selected coins.
- AI and news are currently disabled navigation entries marked as coming soon. They are not part of this polish request.
- Preserve existing functionality and the established visual style. Do not add unnecessary features or introduce a creative redesign.

## Brand Commitments

The existing product name is CoinTracker. Keep its straightforward market-tracking terminology.

## Product Principles

- Make existing browsing and comparison tasks easy to understand and complete.
- Present market values and missing data accurately.
- Keep the interface consistent and usable across screen sizes and both existing themes.
- Prefer focused refinements over expanding product scope.

## Open Decisions

The specific audience, commercial positioning, release plans for AI/news, and any additional language or accessibility requirements remain undecided.
